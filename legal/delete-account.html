Delete your account

Last updated: 12 October 2026

You can delete your TraxJourney account and your data at any time. This page explains how, and what is deleted and what is kept. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

How to delete your account

  • In the app (Android and iOS): open Settings → Delete account and confirm.
  • On the web: sign in at traxjourney.com, open Settings → Delete account and confirm.
  • By email: if you cannot sign in, write to contact@traxjourney.com.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently deletes your account, your trips, photos, memories, journal entries, people, comments and likes, and your Strava and Polarsteps connections, straight away. Deleting your account also revokes our access to your Strava account. If you signed in with Apple, we ask Apple to revoke the token it issued for your account.

If you have a paid plan, deleting your account first cancels it at Stripe immediately, so you are not charged again, and refunds the unused part of the current period if you are still within your withdrawal period (see the Terms of Service). If the cancellation cannot be completed, your account is not deleted and nothing is removed.

What is kept

  • Activities in someone else's trip: activities you add to a trip someone else owns become part of that trip. They belong to its owner and stay in it when you delete your account, unless the trip is encrypted with your key, in which case it is deleted with your account.
  • Refund record: if a refund we owe you could not be made automatically, we keep a minimal record of it (the Stripe identifiers, the amounts, the reason and when each step happened) until you are paid and it is settled, even though your account is deleted.
  • Backups: we back up the database once a day and keep the last 30 backups, so deleted data can remain in a backup for up to 30 days.
  • Server logs and metrics: kept for 30 days.
  • Payment records: Stripe keeps its own records of your payments, as the law requires of it.
